Conductor

Privacy

Last updated November 13, 2026

Conductor is an AI assistant for Logic Pro, in beta. This page explains what the app sends, what we keep, and your rights over it. Conductor is responsible for this data. Questions and requests go to hello@askconductor.ai.

What the app sends

When you send a message, Conductor sends our server:

  • Your message and the conversation so far.
  • Screenshots of Logic Pro's windows. Only windows that belong to Logic, never the rest of your screen.
  • Logic's layout state: the text and controls macOS Accessibility reports for Logic's windows, such as track names, plug-in names and their settings.
  • Your voice, if you use the mic. Each phrase you say is sent as audio to be turned into text, along with any text already in the box.

The app also sends basic usage: message counts, thumbs up or down on replies, the app version, the minutes of voice you use each day and your timezone (so the daily voice limit resets at your midnight), your optional answers to the setup questions, and when you install or uninstall. To count one Mac as one user, it sends a one-way hash of your Mac's hardware ID, never the ID itself. When the app is first set up, we note which country it's in (from its IP address) and where you heard about Conductor (below).

Bug reports and feedback

A bug report you send from the app includes the chat, the app's log of the actions it ran, Logic's window state, and your Logic and Conductor versions. Feedback includes only what you write, unless you tick "Include the chat and logs". Reports never include a screenshot.

The waitlist

When you join the waitlist, we keep your email address, when you signed up, your country, and the site or app that sent you here, if there was one. We use it to let you in, email you the download link, and check it when you set up the app (it works on up to two Macs). When you set up the app with it, we keep the email with that Mac's record, along with where you came from (for example "instagram"), so we can find your data when you ask for a copy or for it to be deleted, and see which places bring people who use Conductor. We keep it while you're on the waitlist or using Conductor, and delete it sooner if you ask. We never email you anything else without asking.

What we keep

  • Screenshots are never stored. They're used to answer that message, then discarded.
  • Voice recordings are never stored. The text they become is kept like any message.
  • We keep your messages, Conductor's replies, usage and reports.
  • We delete it 12 months after it's collected, or sooner if you ask.

How it's used

Only to answer you and to improve Conductor, for example finding where it gave a wrong answer. Your data is never used to train AI models, by us or anyone else. We don't sell it or share it for advertising.

Why we're allowed to use it (the "legal basis" under EU and UK law):

  • To give you the service you asked for: answering your messages, running the actions you approve, your free-message and voice limits, the waitlist and the email that lets you in.
  • Our legitimate interest in running Conductor well: keeping it secure, preventing abuse, fixing bugs, improving answers, and counting website visits and where they come from. You can object to this (see Your rights).
  • Your consent: the chat and logs you choose to include with feedback. You can withdraw consent at any time.

Who else handles it

  • OpenAI generates Conductor's answers, runs its web research, and turns your voice into text. It receives what's needed to answer each message, including the screenshots and voice clips. Under OpenAI's API terms it doesn't train on this data and deletes it within 30 days. See OpenAI's API data policy.
  • Railway hosts our server and database.
  • Resend sends the email that lets you in from the waitlist. It receives your email address.
  • GitHub hosts the app's download, and the app checks it for updates. It sees your IP address and which version you have.
  • Cloudflare hosts this website. Like any web host, it sees your IP address when you visit.

Like any server, ours sees your IP address when the app or this website connects. We use it only to look up your country, with a database on our own server (IP Geolocation by DB-IP), and don't store the address.

Our server and these providers are in the United States, so your data is processed there. Each provider handles it under a data processing agreement with us, which protects data sent from the EU and UK with the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (and the UK's addendum to them).

What stays on your Mac

Your chat history and settings are saved on your Mac in ~/Library/Application Support/Conductor/. Settings → Delete Chat History clears the history, and Settings → Uninstall Conductor deletes the whole folder. Uninstalling doesn't delete what's on our server: for that, email us.

Your rights and deleting your data

Email hello@askconductor.ai from the address you joined the waitlist with and say you'd like your data deleted, or a copy of it. If you write from another address, or set up Conductor before version 0.4.6, include your ID from About Conductor in the app's Conductor menu (Your ID → Copy). We'll delete it from our server within 30 days and confirm by email. To delete it, uninstall Conductor first (Settings → Uninstall Conductor): an installed app starts a new, empty record the next time you use it. Deleted data can remain in our server's backups until they expire, within about 3 months.

You can also ask us, the same way, to correct your data, to limit how we use it, to stop using it for the reasons based on our interest above, or for a copy in a form you can move to another service. We'll answer within a month. If you're in the EU or UK and think we've handled your data wrongly, you can complain to your country's data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).

This website

askconductor.ai uses no cookies and no third-party analytics. To count visits, each page you view sends our server the page, the link's campaign tag (like ?utm_source=instagram), the site that linked you here, your browser type and your country. Your browser keeps a random ID in its local storage so a return visit isn't counted as a new visitor. It isn't tied to your email, your Mac or anything else, clearing your browser's site data removes it, and it's replaced with a new one after 13 months. To keep your browser out of the counts, open askconductor.ai/?notrack once. Visit records are deleted after 12 months.

Children

Conductor isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes what the app sends or how it's used, we'll update this page and the app will ask you to agree again.
